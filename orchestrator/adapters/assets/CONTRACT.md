## Amendment 3 ? error-policy adjudication

The existing CORRUPT_ARTIFACT policy is retryable with max2 attempts; do not change that taxonomy or assert all corruption is terminal. An unapproved URL/redirect is instead INVALID_MANIFEST (terminal) and must be rejected before opening that target; repeated retries of a blocked target are unnecessary. HTTP429 retry count is min(configured max_attempts, rate-limit policy max5), not globally capped by the network policy max3. Missing/malformed/nonfinite Retry-After uses policy backoff; valid nonnegative numeric values are capped60s. Mid-body IO failures remain safely classified WORKER_UNREACHABLE without raw URLs.

Packer tests must permit appended bufferViews for embedded images and must expect data-URI images to become embedded bufferViews, because final registration refuses residual resource URIs. Declared merged buffer byteLength must cover all appended image payloads and source views, with at most3 bytes BIN padding. Existing image.bufferView indices/spans/MIME must be validated. Unsupported buffer-addressing extensions (e.g. EXT_meshopt_compression) must refuse rather than retain incorrect offsets. Malformed shapes/CRC/unsupported ZIP compression produce classified failures.

A provenance manifest must be written BEFORE registry commitment under a unique immutable acquisition path with chosen asset_id and planned destination/hash. No post-registration manifest failure may be presented as an entirely failed acquisition with a durable unannotated Asset. Orphan staging/provenance from a failed attempt may be retained; do not delete/rollback append-only registration. Final duplicate check and registration use existing command lock.
