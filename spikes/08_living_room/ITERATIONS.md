# Render review log

Read PRD sections 4.1 and 4.3 and inspected spike07 baseline.png and comparison.png before implementation. No reference image was supplied to a generative model; all new geometry/materials are procedural.

1. `before-greybox.png`: original synthetic geometry with shared corner mount (-2.48,-1.85,2.25), target(.25,.6,.85), default12mm lens, 3200K profile. Viewed directly. Fails criterion2: bare planes and primitive clutter, strong ceiling band, unexplained bright light pools. 42.588s launch-to-exit; device-wide NVML idle1479.4MiB peak3665.5MiB (+2186.1). EXR conversion initially failed, fixed and retried successfully without another GPU render. First harness version omitted isolated cache/config environment; this run is retained with that limitation, not used for final timing comparison.

2. `living-v1.png`: first procedural room. Viewed directly by orchestrator and independent reviewer. Fails criterion2 despite immediately recognizable sofa/table/TV/lamp/rug. Bookcase looks like solid slab, TV hovers, doorway is false casing on a wall, floor unsegmented. Lamp and ceiling area source were inside closed opaque fixture geometry, suppressing practical pools. Camera ceiling consumes about one third of frame. 39.613s launch-to-exit; NVML idle1547.1MiB peak2647.5MiB (+1100.4). This run records contained cache/config environment in its telemetry.

Requested iteration: actual cased opening and correctly partitioned window wall; open shelves and supported books, grounded furniture and TV support; clipped staggered floor planks; split rounded cushions; visible bulb/shade with actual lights outside opaque caps; lower camera aim (.2,.5,.2), preserving mount/lens. Keep warm domestic pools and cooler window. New before/after pair must both use this final camera.

Telemetry per run is retained under `_work/<run-id>/telemetry.json`; disposable Blender files/logs/EXRs stay there. All memory figures are sampled device occupancy, including other processes; they are not process allocations. Cold single-frame wall times cannot establish sustained cadence.

3. `living-v2.png`: viewed directly. Proper doorway void and furniture support, narrower ceiling band, individual oak boards and split cushions clearly improve the room. Still FAIL criterion2 in orchestrator judgement: sofa seat measured .805m high, very wide dark plank seams, lamp clipping, rubber-like plain rug and blank bookcase side dominating the foreground. Requested a focused third pass on domestic sofa scale, finer seams/grain and fabric weave, rug border, folded curtains, purposeful tabletop detail, shelf orientation, practical placement and hallway depth. 68.625s cold wall time; idle1561.6MiB peak2667.3MiB (+1105.7).
